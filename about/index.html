About Workflow Scout

Workflow Scout is an independent publication about practical AI automation for small businesses.

We compare automation platforms, explain workflow trade-offs, and build practical buying guides using current product documentation and other source-backed evidence.

How we evaluate tools

  • We prioritize current official documentation for pricing, features, limits, and program terms.
  • We separate observed facts from estimates and opinions.
  • We compare operating cost, technical effort, platform dependency, and maintenance burden.
  • We do not treat page views or projections as proof of business value.
  • Affiliate relationships, when they exist, are disclosed and do not determine recommendations.
