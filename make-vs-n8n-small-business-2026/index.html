Make vs n8n in 2026: Which Automation Platform Is Better for Small Businesses?

Updated October 6, 2026. This article currently contains no affiliate links.

Small businesses choosing an automation platform usually face the same trade-off: do you want the fastest path to a working visual workflow, or more technical control and room to customize?

Quick answer

  • Choose Make if you want a visual, no-code-first experience and a low-cost way to test automations.
  • Choose n8n if you are comfortable with a more technical workflow builder and value self-hosting, code steps, custom API calls, or deeper execution control.
  • Do not choose on headline price alone. The two platforms meter usage differently.

Make pricing and usage model

Make currently lists a Free plan at $0 with up to 1,000 credits per month. Its pricing page says the free tier includes the visual workflow builder, routers and filters, thousands of apps, and a 15-minute minimum interval between scheduled runs.

At the 10,000-credit level, Make currently lists Core at $12/month, Pro at $21/month, and Teams at $38/month. Pricing can vary by credit volume and billing options, so verify current figures on the official pricing page before purchasing.

n8n pricing and usage model

n8n Cloud prices paid plans around full workflow executions rather than charging for every step. Its official pricing page currently lists Starter at ?20/month billed annually for 2,500 workflow executions and Pro at ?50/month billed annually for 10,000 executions.

n8n also provides a standard self-hosted Community Edition. Self-hosting offers more control, but you take responsibility for infrastructure, security, updates, monitoring, and troubleshooting.

The key difference: credits vs executions

Make and n8n meter usage differently. Make uses credits tied to scenario actions, while n8n Cloud prices around complete workflow executions regardless of the number of steps inside the workflow. A complex multi-step automation can therefore produce a very different cost comparison from a simple high-volume automation.

Ease of use and technical control

Make is a strong fit when you want a visual SaaS workflow builder without managing infrastructure. n8n is a strong fit when custom API calls, code, self-hosting, or more technical control are important to the workflow.

Affiliate programs

A simple decision process

  1. Write down one workflow you actually need.
  2. Estimate how often it will run.
  3. Count the actions or steps it requires.
  4. Decide whether you are willing to manage hosting.
  5. Build the same small workflow in a free or trial environment.
  6. Compare reliability and maintenance effort, not just subscription price.
